What this covers

This policy explains what French Chunk collects when you use the app or the website, why, and the choices you have. You can use most of French Chunk as a guest without an account.

Information we collect

  • Account details (only if you create an account): your email address and display name. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address and name they share (Apple may give a private relay address) and an account identifier from them. Older email accounts also have a password, stored only as a secure hash.
  • Sign-in and security records: for each sign-in session, the IP address, the device or browser description and the times of sign-in and last activity; a history of sign-ins and failed sign-in attempts. We use these to keep accounts safe and to stop abuse.
  • Your learning data: lessons you mark as done or save, your flashcards and decks, Path test results and badges, learning points and videos you import.
  • Learning activity: how you use the lessons — pages and lessons opened, time actively spent, answers in practice and tests, words you look up, dictation use and settings such as playback speed. Guests are recorded with a random device identifier, not a name; if you later sign in on that device, this activity joins your account. We use it to show your progress and to improve the lessons and how they are taught.
  • Data kept on your device: the lesson library, a copy of your learning data and your settings are stored on your device so the app opens quickly and works offline. In the app, your sign-in token is kept in the device's secure storage.

Advertising (app only)

The app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob unless you are a member. Before any ad, the app asks for your consent where the law requires it (Google's consent form) and, on iPhone, asks whether you allow tracking. If you do not allow it, ads are not personalised with your device's advertising identifier. Google processes ad data under its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. The website has no ads.

Membership and payments

The ad-free membership is a subscription sold and billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment card or billing details; the app only asks the store whether your membership is active. You manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account.

Other services

  • YouTube: video lessons play in YouTube's embedded player (and some in PeerTube players of French education services), which may collect data under their own policies.
  • Google Fonts on the website, and our hosting provider, which stores the data above on our server.
  • Lesson content is written with the help of AI services; your account and learning data are not sent to them. When you import a video, only its link and its subtitles are used to make the lesson.

How we use it — and what we never do

We use this information to run French Chunk, keep your progress in sync across your devices, keep accounts secure, and understand how learners study so we can improve the lessons. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it except with the services named above or when required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Me → Delete account): your account, learning data and learning activity are then deleted for good. Signing out removes the copy kept on that device. Guest activity is linked only to a random device identifier.

Children

French Chunk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. For questions or requests about your data, contact us through the support details on French Chunk's App Store or Google Play page.

適用範圍

本政策說明你使用 French Chunk 應用程式或網站時，我們收集甚麼資料、用途，以及你的選擇。大部分功能毋須帳號，以訪客身份即可使用。

我們收集的資料

  • 帳號資料（只在你建立帳號時）：電郵地址和顯示名稱。如你以 Apple 或 Google 登入，我們會收到他們提供的電郵地址和名稱（Apple 可能提供轉寄用的私人電郵）及其帳號識別碼。較早以電郵建立的帳號另有密碼，只以安全雜湊值儲存。
  • 登入及安全紀錄：每個登入工作階段的 IP 位址、裝置或瀏覽器描述、登入及最後活動時間；登入及登入失敗紀錄。用於保障帳號安全及防止濫用。
  • 你的學習資料：標示為完成或收藏的課程、單字卡及分類、學習路徑測驗成績及徽章、學習點數及你匯入的影片。
  • 學習活動：你如何使用課程——開啟的頁面及課程、實際學習時間、練習及測驗的答案、查詢的字詞、默書使用情況及播放速度等設定。訪客以隨機裝置識別碼記錄，不含姓名；你其後在該裝置登入，相關活動會併入你的帳號。用於顯示你的進度，以及改善課程內容和教學方式。
  • 儲存在你裝置上的資料：課程庫、你的學習資料副本及設定會存於裝置，讓應用程式開得更快並可離線使用。應用程式的登入憑證存於裝置的安全儲存區。

廣告（只限應用程式）

除非你是會員，應用程式會顯示 Google AdMob 橫幅廣告。顯示廣告前，應用程式會在法律要求的地區徵求你同意（Google 的同意表格），在 iPhone 上亦會詢問你是否允許追蹤；如你不允許，廣告不會使用裝置的廣告識別碼作個人化。Google 按其政策處理廣告資料：policies.google.com/technologies/ads。網站沒有廣告。

會員及付款

免廣告會員是由 Apple（App Store）或 Google（Google Play）銷售及收費的訂閱。我們不會看到你的付款卡或帳單資料；應用程式只會向商店查詢你的會員是否有效。你可在 App Store 或 Google Play 帳號管理或取消訂閱。

其他服務

  • YouTube：影片課程以 YouTube 嵌入式播放器播放（部分以法國教育 PeerTube 服務的播放器播放），這些服務可能按其政策收集資料。
  • 網站使用 Google Fonts；我們的伺服器託管服務商會儲存上述資料。
  • 課程內容由 AI 服務協助編寫；你的帳號及學習資料不會傳送給它們。你匯入影片時，只會使用影片連結及字幕製作課程。

用途——以及我們絕不會做的事

我們使用這些資料來營運 French Chunk、在你的裝置之間同步進度、保障帳號安全，以及了解學員的學習方式以改善課程。我們不會出售你的個人資料；除上述服務或法律要求外，亦不會與他人分享。

保存及刪除

帳號存在期間，我們會保存你的帳號資料。你可隨時在應用程式刪除帳號（我 → 刪除帳號），你的帳號、學習資料及學習活動會被永久刪除。登出會刪除該裝置上的副本。訪客活動只與隨機裝置識別碼相關。

兒童

French Chunk 並非為 13 歲以下兒童而設，我們不會在知情下收集他們的個人資料。

政策更新及聯絡

如本政策有更改，我們會更新本頁及日期。如對你的資料有疑問或要求，請透過 French Chunk 在 App Store 或 Google Play 頁面上的支援資料聯絡我們。
