Common questions

How do I sign in?

Open the Me tab and choose Sign in with Apple or Continue with Google. Accounts made earlier with an email and password can still use Log in with email. You can use most of the app as a guest; an account keeps your saved lessons, Path progress and flashcards on all your devices.

How do I remove ads?

Become a member on the Me tab. Membership is an auto-renewing monthly subscription billed by the App Store or Google Play, and it renews until you cancel.

How do I cancel or manage my membership?

On iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → French Chunk, or Manage subscription on the app's Me tab. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

I paid but still see ads.

Tap Restore purchases on the Me tab while you are online, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with.

I can't hear the French audio.

Turn off silent mode and check the volume. The app speaks with your device's French (France) voice: on iPhone go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → French and download a France voice.

A video doesn't play.

Video lessons play from YouTube and need an internet connection. If a video stays black, try again later or open another lesson, and tell us which one.

How do I delete my account?

On the Me tab, tap Delete account and confirm. Your account, saved lessons, progress and flashcards are deleted for good. Deleting the account does not cancel a store subscription — cancel it as described above.

常見問題

怎樣登入？

打開「我」分頁，選擇 以 Apple 登入 或 以 Google 繼續。之前用電郵和密碼建立的帳號，仍可用「以電郵登入」。大部分功能不登入也能使用；登入後，收藏、學習路徑進度和單字卡會在所有裝置同步。

怎樣移除廣告？

在「我」分頁加入會員。會員是自動續訂的月費訂閱，由 App Store 或 Google Play 收費，取消前會每月自動續訂。

怎樣取消或管理會員？

iPhone：設定 → [你的名字] → 訂閱項目 → French Chunk，或在 app「我」分頁按 管理訂閱。Android：Google Play → 付款和訂閱 → 訂閱。請在本期結束前至少 24 小時取消，以免被收取下一期費用。退款由 Apple 或 Google 處理。

已付款但仍看到廣告

在連上網絡、並登入付款時所用的 Apple ID 或 Google 帳戶的情況下，按「我」分頁的 恢復購買。

聽不到法語發音

請關閉靜音並調高音量。app 使用裝置內建的法語（法國）語音：iPhone 請到 設定 → 輔助使用 → 語音內容 → 聲音 → 法文，下載一個法國的語音。

影片無法播放

影片課程由 YouTube 播放，需要連上網絡。如果影片一直是黑畫面，請稍後再試或先看其他課程，並告訴我們是哪一課。

怎樣刪除帳號？

在「我」分頁按 刪除帳號 並確認。帳號、收藏、進度和單字卡會被永久刪除。刪除帳號不會取消商店訂閱，請按上面的方法取消。
